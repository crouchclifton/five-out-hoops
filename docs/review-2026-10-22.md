# Fable 5.1 review, October 22, 2026

A read-only review of the whole game by Fable 5.1, given the code, the transcript of Coach Russ's video and screenshots of every screen. Kept verbatim below. Clifton's decisions on it are in `PLAN.md`.

One correction from checking the code: in Dribble Dash, any kid who beats the defender gets at least bronze (cpuTime is 11 to 12.2 s, and bronze is 13 s or less). The real issue is that slow tappers can't beat the defender, not that bronze is unreachable.

---

Overall: the basketball is right. `rotation()` (js/lessons.js:23) reproduces all four rotations in Coach Russ's video (top to wing, wing to corner, corner to wing, wing to top), and the coach-notes table in index.html matches too. The lesson flow is solid and I found no way to hard-lock a session. The biggest problems are about whether kids are learning the *read* or just a symbol, three bonus games tuned too hard for 7 to 9 year olds, and progress that can silently vanish on iPhone.

## (a) Real bugs

**1. Progress does not carry from Safari into the home-screen app (high confidence).** On iOS, a home-screen web app has its own storage, separate from Safari. A kid who does three lessons in Safari, then follows the "Add to Home Screen" sheet (js/app.js:273), opens the icon and sees zero stars, zero XP. Also, Safari deletes a site's localStorage after about 7 days without a visit when it is not installed; `navigator.storage.persist()` (app.js:269) does nothing on Safari. Fix: show the install sheet *before* the first lesson on iOS Safari (not just a button at the bottom), and say plainly on it that stars start over in the new icon. Longer term, add an "export/import code" or a tiny backup on the coach's side.

**2. Coach lines get cut off mid-sentence (high confidence).** `say()` fires a clip and returns at once; the script then sleeps a fixed time that is shorter than the clip. Examples: lessons.js:325 "Smart! The defender is in the way, so we pass." (about 3 s of speech) is followed by `sleep(1100)`, then the pass question's `say()` replaces it. Same at :388 ("Good eyes!", 1000 ms), :316/:379 narration, and lesson 2's "Everyone fills the open spot" (:425) which is cut by the next round's prompt. For non-readers the voice *is* the lesson, so a clipped sentence is lost teaching. Fix: make `say()` return a promise that resolves on the audio `ended` event (fall back to a length-based timer when sound is off) and `await` it for informational lines; keep questions interruptible.

**3. Service worker never re-caches new voice clips, and a flaky gym connection stalls the load (high confidence).** sw.js has one cache name with no version; voice clips are precached only during `install`, which only runs when sw.js itself changes. After a text edit, new clips are fetched only when played online; offline, those lines are silent. Also, code is network-first with no timeout (sw.js:57), so on one-bar Wi-Fi the app can sit on a blank screen for a long time instead of using the cache. Fix: put a build stamp in sw.js on every deploy and delete old caches on `activate`; wrap the shell fetch in a 3 s timeout that falls back to cache.

**4. Dribble Dash bronze is unreachable, and slow tappers can never medal (high confidence).** minigames.js:28 tiers are 13/10/8 s but `cpuTime` is 11 to 12.2 s (:418), and `medalFor` returns 0 when the defender wins. Finishing needs 42 alternating taps (`.024` per tap, :434), so winning needs over 3.5 taps/s and gold needs 5.3/s. Many 7 year olds will lose every time and see "Defender wins!" with no path forward. Fix: cpuTime 15 to 17 s, tiers 16/12/9, or make the defender's speed adapt to the kid's best time.

**5. Free Throw Frenzy green zone shrinks to a 90 ms window (high confidence).** Zone width multiplies by .86 per make and needle speed rises .13 per make (:232, :262). After 5 makes the window is about 0.14 of the bar at 1.5 sweeps/s, roughly 94 ms, which is below a child's reaction time. Silver (15 pts) needs 7 makes. Fix: floor `zoneW` at .18, cap speed around 1.2, or shrink by .93.

**6. Stale coach greeting on the home screen (medium confidence that kids hit it often; code path is certain).** `renderHome(false)` (used after quitting a bonus game, notes, and watch mode) leaves `#heroBubble` as it was, so a player with two finished lessons can see "Tap the green circle to start your first lesson!" (screenshot 09 shows exactly this). Fix: always recompute the bubble text; only skip *speaking* it.

**7. Minor:** "Total XP" card on lesson complete (app.js:183) shows XP earned this lesson, not the total in the top bar; label it "XP earned". Streak only counts lessons (`touchStreak` in `lessonComplete`), not bonus games; decide and say so. The DST day can break a streak because `now - 864e5` may not be "yesterday" (core.js:108); use calendar math.

## (b) Teaching problems

**8. Kids learn to look for the 💤, not for the defender.** Every "open" read in lessons 4, 5 and 6 shows a zzz on the defender (lessons.js:153), including the Big Game. On a real court there is no zzz. The spatial read ("is my defender between me and the hoop?") is what transfers. Fix: keep the zzz in lesson 4 and 5's first rounds, then fade it (a `hideZ` flag for later rounds and all of lesson 6). For give-and-go, show the cutter running *in front of* a defender who trails, as the video teaches, instead of a defender frozen asleep.

**9. Rule numbers arrive out of order.** Kids hear "Rule #2" in lesson 2, "Rule #3" in lesson 3, and "Rule #1" in lesson 5. A 7 year old will ask where Rule #1 went. Either state all three rules in lesson 1's intro (10 seconds, spoken), or drop the numbers from the lesson intros and only number them in the Big Game recap.

**10. Pass Pop rewards the skip pass the lessons just forbade.** `pickOpen` (minigames.js:329) picks any of the four teammates, so "OPEN!" lights up on the far corner from the top and the kid is paid +1 for a pass the lessons call a steal. Restrict candidates to `PASSES[holder.spot]`, and the game becomes a reinforcement drill instead of a contradiction.

**11. Lesson 2's cut question is the same binary five times.** "Stand still" vs "Cut to the hoop" (lessons.js:351) has no decision after round one. Try: rounds 1 and 2 as is, rounds 3 to 5 remove the buttons and require tapping the hoop zone on the court, so the kid acts out the cut instead of picking a label.

**12. Big Game points are not under the kid's control.** Points come only from the random `open` events (lessons.js:580 to 581); a wrong answer costs nothing but a retry. "Score as many points as you can" is a promise the game cannot keep, and sharper kids will notice. Fix: a wrong first answer becomes a turnover (defense gets the ball, no points that turn), or award +1 for each first-try correct read, so the score reflects reads.

**13. Corner drives.** After rotations the ball can sit in a corner and the game can say "Attack!" from there. The video explicitly cautions against corner drives (baseline is an extra defender). Small, but for faithfulness make `drive: "open"` only when the holder is at top or wing.

## (c) Game design

**14. Swish Shot asks for lead-shooting at a moving target.** Hoop swings at 0.8 rad/s with amplitude .28W and the hit test uses the hoop's position at landing 0.8 s later (:138, :175), so the hoop can move about 70 px during flight versus a 47 px hit window. A straight-up flick only scores when the hoop happens to pass center. Bronze (3 makes in 30 s) is OK; silver and gold will feel random. Fix: judge the hit against the hoop position at launch, or slow the swing to 0.4 rad/s, or widen the window to `.5 * hoopW`.

**15. Reasons to come back next week are thin.** After six lessons the path is done, XP keeps climbing by replaying, and medals are permanent. Cheap additions that fit the Duolingo model: a daily "3 quick reads" challenge (random drive/pass and give-and-go reads with the zzz hidden) that is the only thing that keeps the streak alive, personal-best chasing on bonus games surfaced on the home screen, and a "watch with your team" mode that pauses and asks the group.

**16. Lesson length is good** (10 to 14 questions, 2 to 3 minutes) except the Big Game, which is 8 turns at up to 3 questions each, with no mid-lesson save and a quit that loses it all. Consider 6 turns, or save per-turn progress.

## (d) Nice-to-haves

- Speak the first greeting on the first tap (it is silent because audio is not yet unlocked, core.js:83 and app.js:45), so a non-reader hears "tap the green circle".
- Lesson 1 part 2 still draws the four inside-the-line decoys as dashed "spots" after telling kids there are only five spots; dim them or draw them differently.
- `lessonComplete` awards XP on every replay; fine for kids, but it makes XP mean less than stars.
- Dribble Dash wrong-side taps are free; a small time penalty would teach rhythm.
- Unreachable but harmless: the voice set has no clip for "32 points"; the real maximum is 16.
