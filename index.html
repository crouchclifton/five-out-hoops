For coaches and parents

Made by Coach Clifton for his Eagles team in the Southern Madison Future Eagles league, so players can learn the offense at home. It's a team project, not an official league app.

This game teaches the basics of the 5-Out motion offense, following Coach Russ's video "5-Out Motion Offense: A Complete Guide for Coaches".

Setup: all five players start outside the 3-point line. One at the top, two on the wings, two in the corners. It is positionless, so anyone can start anywhere.

The three rules: (1) attack the basket whenever you can, (2) pass and move (never stand still after a pass), (3) fill the open spot.

PassWhat happens
Top to wingPasser cuts to the hoop looking for the give and go, then goes to the opposite corner. The other wing fills the top, and that corner fills the wing.
Wing to cornerPasser cuts, then goes to the opposite corner. Top fills the wing, other wing fills the top, other corner fills that wing.
Corner to wingPasser cuts, then comes back to the same corner.
Wing to topPasser cuts, then goes to the same-side corner. That corner moves up to fill the wing.

How it plays: eleven lessons on a path in three units, each unlocking the next. Lessons 1 to 5, 7 and 8 each unlock a bonus game (Swish Shot, Pass Pop, Free Throw Frenzy, Dribble Dash, Hoop Wall, Rotation Rush, Cutter Catch), and the rest let players pick one. Rotation Rush and Cutter Catch practice the lesson before them: where the passer ends up, and passing to a cutter once he is in front of his defender. Unit 3, the Practice Court, reviews the same material a different way: where the passer goes after each kind of pass, stepping away before cutting in front, corner smarts, spotting a teammate's mistake, and a fast Lightning Round with no spot names. Everything in the game stays inside the first 6:10 of the video (setup, the three rules and the four rotations). Players earn XP, stars and a daily streak (a lesson or a bonus game keeps it going). Finishing a lesson the first time, a new best medal, the daily Skills Challenges and streak milestones earn basketball cards of made-up cartoon players (48 to collect), kept in the Card Binder; practicing a lesson again earns one card a day, and extra copies turn into XP. There are no lives to lose, so nobody gets stopped out. "Watch the 5-Out" plays the offense by itself with narration, good for a team huddle around one phone.

Left out on purpose for 7 to 9 year olds: screens away, on-ball screens, backdoor cuts and post seals. The video covers them once the basic movement is solid. Skip passes (top to corner, wing to wing) count as mistakes, to build the habit of passing to the teammate next to you.

Players and privacy: each player is a face, a jersey and a number. No names are asked for. Players and their progress are saved only on this phone; the game has no server and sends nothing anywhere. To start one player over, tap the player in the top left corner of the home screen.
