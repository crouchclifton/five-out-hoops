For coaches and parents

This game teaches the basics of the 5-Out motion offense, following Coach Russ's video "5-Out Motion Offense: A Complete Guide for Coaches".

Setup: all five players start outside the 3-point line. One at the top, two on the wings, two in the corners. It is positionless, so anyone can start anywhere.

The three rules: (1) attack the basket whenever you can, (2) pass and move (never stand still after a pass), (3) fill the open spot.

PassWhat happens
Top to wingPasser cuts to the hoop looking for the give and go, then goes to the opposite corner. The other wing fills the top, and that corner fills the wing.
Wing to cornerPasser cuts, then goes to the opposite corner. Top fills the wing, other wing fills the top, other corner fills that wing.
Corner to wingPasser cuts, then comes back to the same corner.
Wing to topPasser cuts, then goes to the same-side corner. That corner moves up to fill the wing.

How it plays: six lessons on a path, each unlocking the next. Lessons 1 to 4 each unlock a bonus game (Swish Shot, Pass Pop, Free Throw Frenzy, Dribble Dash), and lessons 5 and 6 let players pick one. Players earn XP, stars and a daily streak. There are no lives to lose, so nobody gets stopped out. "Watch the 5-Out" plays the offense by itself with narration, good for a team huddle around one phone.

Left out on purpose for 7 to 9 year olds: screens away, on-ball screens, backdoor cuts and post seals. The video covers them once the basic movement is solid. Skip passes (top to corner, wing to wing) count as mistakes, to build the habit of passing to the teammate next to you.
